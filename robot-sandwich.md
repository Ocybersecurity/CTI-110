
# Robot sandwich how to guide

## Assumptions
- Bread is out and on a plate two slices.
- Jar of peanut butter is out and lid is off.
- Jar of jelly is out and lid is off.
- Butter knife is laying next to the plate.

## Steps to complete making a sandwich
1. Pick up butter knife from the dull end.
2. Stick sharper end of the butter knife into the opened end of the peanut butter jar.
3. Pull out butter knife from jar in a down up forward motion.
4. Spread peanut butter from the butter knife on one side of one of the slices of bread on the plate.
5. Stick the sharper end of the butter knife into the opened end of the jelly jar.
6. Pull out butter knife from jar in a down up forward motion.
7. Spread jelly from the butter knife on the one side of the slice bread thats on the plate that the peanut butter is not on.
8. Put the butter knife down onto the side of the plate next to one of the bread slices.
9. Pickup both slices and put them together placing both the jelly and peanut butter sides together.
10. Place the joined slices down onto the plate.
11. Pick up butter knife from the dull end that is on the plate.
12. Slice the peanut butter sandwich in half evenly straight down the middle with the sharper end of the butter knife.
13. Put the butter knife down on the plate next to the peanut butter sandwich.

## Done When butter knife is placed down on the plate.
